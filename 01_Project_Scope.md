# Project Scope

## 1. Project Background

The construction of underground metro infrastructure requires substantial quantities of concrete, reinforcing steel, and other materials, resulting in significant embodied greenhouse gas emissions.

This project investigates how material selection and transportation strategies can reduce construction-related carbon emissions for a hypothetical underground metro station in Copenhagen.

## 2. Problem Statement

Conventional construction materials contribute to the carbon footprint of metro infrastructure. However, alternative materials may offer lower environmental impacts at different costs.

The challenge is to quantify baseline emissions, identify major emission sources, and evaluate feasible carbon reduction strategies using a transparent and reproducible calculation framework.

## 3. Project Objectives

1. Develop a baseline carbon footprint model covering life-cycle modules A1–A5.
2. Quantify the contribution of major construction materials, particularly concrete and reinforcing steel.
3. Compare conventional and low-carbon material alternatives using Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs).
4. Evaluate transportation-related emissions and circular economy opportunities.
5. Analyze carbon reduction potential and estimated cost trade-offs.
6. Communicate results through Excel, Python, Power BI, and a technical report.

## 4. Research Questions

- RQ1: What is the estimated A1–A5 carbon footprint of the hypothetical metro station?
- RQ2: Which construction materials contribute most to total emissions?
- RQ3: How much can emissions be reduced through alternative concrete and steel products?
- RQ4: How do transportation assumptions affect the results?
- RQ5: What are the estimated cost implications of carbon reduction strategies?

## 5. Proposed Scenarios

| Scenario | Description |
|---|---|
| S0 | Baseline construction materials |
| S1 | Low-carbon concrete alternative |
| S2 | Alternative reinforcing steel |
| S3 | Combined low-carbon materials and transportation improvements |

## 6. Expected Deliverables

- Structured construction material and EPD dataset
- Excel-based carbon calculation workbook
- Python-based LCA and scenario analysis
- Comparative carbon and cost analysis
- Power BI sustainability dashboard
- Final technical report with recommendations

## 7. Project Limitations

- The case study represents a hypothetical station rather than an actual Metroselskabet project.
- Construction quantities will be synthetic and documented.
- The primary assessment boundary is A1–A5.
- Operational emissions and end-of-life stages are excluded from the primary assessment.
- Cost estimates will depend on data availability and stated assumptions.
- Results are intended for comparative analysis rather than verified project-level environmental reporting.
